# Patcy AISec — services, deliverables, and pricing

We help teams ship AI agents that are safe to put in front of customers and internal users. Four services:

**1. AI Agent Security Audit / Red-Team — from $4,500.** We attack your AI system the way a real adversary would — prompt injection (direct and indirect), secret and data extraction, jailbreaks, excessive-agency and tool-abuse tests, RAG/retrieval poisoning, and denial-of-wallet. You get a written findings report: every issue mapped to the OWASP LLM Top 10, MITRE ATLAS, and a CWE, severity-rated, with a concrete remediation for each and a re-test after you fix. Typical turnaround: 1–2 weeks.

**2. Secure AI Agent Build — from $6,000.** We design and build a production RAG agent or copilot with security in from the start: least privilege on tools, data-minimized prompts (no secrets in context), input/output guardrails, retrieval sanitization, human-in-the-loop on consequential actions, and an evidence-producing test suite. You get the code, the architecture, and the assessment.

**3. Guardrail Engineering — from $3,000.** We add input/output guardrails to an existing agent — prompt-injection filtering (LLM Guard, Rebuff, Meta Prompt Guard), output/DLP scanning (Llama Guard, Presidio), context sanitization ("spotlighting"), and rate/size limits — and, crucially, we tell you where each control's limits are and what still needs an architectural fix.

**4. AI Security Training — $1,500 / session.** A hands-on team workshop on the OWASP LLM Top 10 and safe AI deployment, built around live break-and-fix labs (like the one you may be talking to right now).

**Ongoing advisory** is available as a monthly retainer from $2,000/month for teams shipping AI continuously.

**What every engagement includes:** a written report, a prioritized remediation plan, standards mappings (OWASP LLM Top 10, NIST AI RMF, MITRE ATLAS), and a re-test to confirm fixes. We always sign an NDA before we start. To scope your project, book a free 30-minute call at hello@patcyaisec.com.
